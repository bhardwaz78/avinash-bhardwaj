What is NOTHING about?

NOTHING is a novel about love, identity, and the silence that remains. Its back cover describes it as a deeply personal exploration of love, insecurity, identity, and the quiet destruction that happens when we build ourselves around someone else. Avinash wrote and edited it himself at the age of seventeen.

Is NOTHING a novel or a memoir?

It is published as a novel. Its front cover reads 'A novel about love, identity, and the silence that remains.'

What is The Woman Behind the Woman about?

A first-person inquiry into women, relationships, money, culture and law, and the things people are afraid to say about them. Its back cover lists influence and manipulation, feminism and its limits, money and marriage, law and consequence, and the Indian women we inherited from stories. It is an inquiry in his own voice, not an academic study.

How does The Woman Behind the Woman separate opinion from evidence?

Its back cover says the author separates what he has observed, what he concludes, and what can be checked against a source, and that he argues with both extremes and is willing to be wrong in public. It also says the book is not a verdict on women but a conversation difficult enough that neither side can win by shouting.

Why is The Woman Behind the Woman written in the first person?

Because it is an inquiry, not a neutral survey. Avinash writes it as his own argument and experience so that readers can disagree with him directly.
